Luz: blonde messy ponytail, blue eyes, oversized dark navy school jacket over white shirt and thin dark blue tie, loose navy pants, gray/white sneakers, dark navy backpack. STRICT CELL FIT: every pixel of each frame including hair and ruler must stay at least 16 px inside its own cell, with no crossing into neighbors. Keep same character scale and same lowest-shoe baseline in all eight frames. Pose arc: 0-1 coiled compact crouch, ruler held back close to hip; 2 initiate shoulder/hip rotation; 3 CONTACT, a clear low flat horizontal cut at knee/low-waist height with the arm extended to the right and ruler straight horizontal; 4-5 low horizontal follow-through; 6-7 return to the same planted crouch. Transparent background only; absolutely no glow, haze, vignette, drop shadow, colored backdrop, floor, smear, text, frame numbers, or grid lines. The image will be sliced mechanically, so center each character in its cell leaving room on the right for ruler extension. Preserve correct anatomy and consistent face, outfit, backpack, proportions, line weight, and scale.
